### Five starting points

Explore requirements for integrations, evaluation and potential pilots; outside-vendor onboarding is not yet established.
- **[Ayman Itani](https://github.com/loaderxxx/dubai-future-week-atlas-2026/blob/research/2026-11-18-atlas/research/people/ayman-itani.md)** — practical Claude adoption and the *Claude As Your Co-Founder* workshop.
- **[Kanwal Aalijah](https://github.com/loaderxxx/dubai-future-week-atlas-2026/blob/research/2026-11-18-atlas/research/people/kanwal-aalijah.md)** — AI programmes and the path from prototype to a system that can be evaluated.
- **[Tom Gruber](https://github.com/loaderxxx/dubai-future-week-atlas-2026/blob/research/2026-11-18-atlas/research/people/tom-gruber.md)** — personal AI, user control and trust.

These are editorial recommendations, not confirmed meetings, buying intent or endorsements. Check the programme and access conditions before attending.

### Trust, access and privacy

- Sources and evidence gaps belong with each profile. Product claims are not independent performance tests.
- Public sessions can still require separate registration or approval. Invite-only sessions are not open-access recommendations.
- This public repository excludes private conversations, personal contact databases, credentials and registration details.
- No outreach has been sent as part of this atlas.
- Found an error or a better primary source? [Open an issue](https://github.com/loaderxxx/dubai-future-week-atlas-2026/issues) with the relevant page and source, without personal or confidential information.

### Что особенно интересно

**Chamber AI Hub** — применение агентов в бизнес-процессах; **Ayman Itani** — практическое внедрение Claude; **Kanwal Aalijah** — оценка AI-систем; **Tom Gruber** — персональные агенты и доверие; **Litmus** — измеримая проверка приватности моделей. Это редакционный выбор, не обещание встречи или сотрудничества.

Для предметного разговора:
> Какой ручной процесс сейчас отнимает больше всего времени? Что уже работает, а что остаётся прототипом? Какой результат ограниченного пилота вы сочли бы успешным?
