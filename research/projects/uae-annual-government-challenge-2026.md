# UAE Annual Government Challenge 2026 / Государственный конкурс агентного AI

Reviewed: 18 November 2026. Public-source research; incomplete rules. No application submitted.

## Verified facts / Проверенные факты

The Annual Government Challenge was announced on 2 November 2026. It is overseen by the Mohammed bin Rashid Centre for Government Innovation, Ministry of Cabinet Affairs. Its first edition covers Agentic AI.
- Eligibility stated: federal ministries, entities and their employees.
- Three tracks: Government Services, Internal Operations, Unconventional Ideas.
- Total prizes: AED 9 million across nine winning projects. Individual allocations are not established.
- Evaluation includes feasibility, strategic fit, measurable impact, scalability and governance.
- Registration: [challenge.gov.ae](https://challenge.gov.ae/).

Это федеральный конкурс, а не автоматически открытая программа для частных стартапов. Общий фонд — 9 млн дирхамов на девять победителей. Направления: государственные услуги, внутренние процессы, нестандартные идеи. Куратор — MBRCGI при Ministry of Cabinet Affairs.

Source: [Dubai Protocol Department announcement, 2 November 2026](https://protocol.dubai.ae/en/media-listing/news-events/mohammed-bin-rashid-launches-annual-government-challenge-for-federal-employees-teams/).

## Potential fit — analysis, not an eligibility decision

For an agent-orchestration prototype such as Lyra Queen, the strongest discussion route would be a tightly scoped **Internal Operations** pilot, conditional on an eligible government partner and explicit permission:
1. Intake and classification of requests.
2. Source-linked retrieval from approved documents.
3. Human approval before consequential actions.
4. Audit trail, access isolation and error escalation.
5. Measured baseline versus assisted workflow: turnaround time, accuracy, cost and intervention rate.

A general orchestration demo is not proof of production readiness. Start with synthetic data and one measurable task; do not use private citizen records in a public demo. A website/music product alone would need a clearly evidenced government use case.

Для Lyra Queen логично обсуждать ограниченный пилот внутренних процессов, а не «автоматизацию всего государства». Для Lyra CRM возможна адаптация интерфейса приёма запросов, но это гипотеза и новая разработка, не подтверждённый готовый государственный продукт. Lyra Music / Signature не являются прямым соответствием лишь по наличию AI.

## Separate opportunities — do not confuse competitions

- [DMCC × JetBrains Agentic AI Challenge](https://landing.dmcc.ae/dmcc-x-jetbrains-ai-challenge-2026): USD 40,000+ prize pool; published deadline 30 November 2026 at 12:00 am GST. [Open Track](https://landing.dmcc.ae/open-agentic-ai-track) accepts incorporated companies worldwide and solo founders with a working MVP, beta or live product, up to 250 employees. Treat the midnight wording cautiously and submit earlier if pursuing it.
- [Create AI Agents Championship](https://myhub.dubaichamber.com/en/web/dubai-chamber-of-digital-economy/create-ai-agents): a different Dubai Chambers competition. [Official WAM launch](https://www.wam.ae/en/article/c2mg2qf-hamdan-bin-mohammed-launches-create-agents) reports a pool above AED 2.5 million and December 2026 registration close; exact day must be checked.
- These are alternative research leads, not applications or commitments. Verify current full rules before submission.
