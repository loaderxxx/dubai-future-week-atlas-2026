## English

### Identity: what the names establish

The Prototypes for Humanity catalogue calls its 2026 entry **“Space, Medicine, Emulsion”** and explicitly describes **TIGERS-X** in the project text. It lists **Chulalongkorn University, Thailand**, and **Thakdanai Sirisombat** under Team. This supports a connection between the names, but not sole authorship or leadership of the wider programme. [S1]

A separate institutional account from **Chulabhorn Royal Academy (CRA)** names **Wares Chancharoen** as TIGERS-X’s lead project developer. **Chulalongkorn University and Chulabhorn Royal Academy are different institutions.** Their detailed division of responsibilities, and Thakdanai’s specific contribution, remain unverified here. [S2]

### What it does

The exhibition describes a modular controller for compact scientific payloads, with electronics, fluid containment and remote monitoring. It links the design to an architecture used on the ISS and an adaptation for preparing intravenous nutrition. These are the project’s published claims, not independently tested capabilities. [S1]

CRA’s programme page describes microgravity research into emulsification, using a remotely operated, Thai-built lab-on-a-chip payload. It gives dimensions of **20 × 20 × 10 cm** and describes returning experimental video to Earth. The page combines scientific aims and planned procedures; it is not a complete results dataset. [S3]

CRA’s **29 May 2026** news report states that the ISS experiment was activated on **26 May 2026** and that initial data reached the ground team. This establishes an institutional report of operation, not independent validation of performance or clinical readiness. [S2]

### What is and is not supported

| Supported by the cited sources | Not established |
|---|---|
| A catalogue link between TIGERS-X and “Space, Medicine, Emulsion” | That the exhibition entry and the entire programme have identical scope |
| A named exhibition team member and a separately named programme lead | Sole invention, ownership, complete contributor list or licensing rights |
| Published claims about space experiments and a healthcare adaptation | Regulatory clearance, hospital deployment, manufacturing readiness or measured cost savings |

### Why this is worth discussing

**Our interpretation:** the useful connection is how constrained hardware, remote operation and experimental evidence move between space research and a terrestrial application. For COSMOSYNTH, this suggests questions about control interfaces, telemetry, reproducibility and integration boundaries, rather than assuming a ready-to-deploy partnership.

For AI work, possible topics include searchable experiment documentation and analysis of non-sensitive telemetry. These are discussion ideas, not claims that the team needs, accepts or has authorised such work.

### Questions for the team

1. What exactly does the exhibition prototype include, and how does it relate to the CRA flight programme?
2. Who owns the control electronics, experiment design and terrestrial adaptation?
3. Which flight results, validation reports and interface documents can be shared publicly?
4. What is implemented today, what remains a research goal, and how is success measured?
5. Is there a non-clinical software or documentation task suitable for a bounded collaboration?

Do not infer anyone’s identity, employment or participation from a phone number or an informal encounter. This profile contains no private contact details and records no meeting or partnership.

## Русский

### Названия и роли

В каталоге Prototypes for Humanity проект называется **«Space, Medicine, Emulsion»**; описание прямо упоминает **TIGERS-X**. В карточке указаны **Chulalongkorn University, Таиланд**, и **Thakdanai Sirisombat** в разделе команды. Это не подтверждение его единоличного авторства или руководства всей программой. [S1]

**Chulabhorn Royal Academy (CRA)** отдельно называет ведущим разработчиком TIGERS-X **Wares Chancharoen**. Это другое учреждение. Распределение работ и конкретную роль Thakdanai необходимо уточнить у команды. [S2]

### Предмет исследования

Выставочная карточка связывает управление компактной космической полезной нагрузкой с адаптацией для приготовления внутривенного питания. [S1] CRA описывает дистанционный эксперимент с эмульсиями в микрогравитации и компактный lab-on-a-chip. [S3] В новости от 29 мая 2026 сообщается о запуске эксперимента на МКС 26 мая и получении первых данных. [S2]

Это подтверждения опубликованных заявлений организаций, а не независимый аудит. Не установлены медицинские разрешения, готовность серийного изделия, полная структура команды и права на технологии.

### Что обсудить

Наш интерес для COSMOSYNTH — интерфейсы управления, телеметрия, проверяемость экспериментов и перенос инженерных решений между космосом и наземными задачами. Возможные AI-направления следует обсуждать только после уточнения потребностей и доступных несекретных данных.
