# Organizations and prototype projects: verified first atlas

Checked 18 November 2026. Public professional data only. No outreach or publication performed.

## Coverage
- `dfw-companies-organizations.csv`: 29 organization records, comprising the eight announced Concept Partners and selected organizers, universities and programme collaborators. This is NOT an exhaustive exhibitor/company list.
- `dfw-companies-prototypes-2026.csv`: **100 unique 2026 projects**, exactly matching the exhibition's announced cohort count. Public catalogue pages 1–18 were checked; pages 1–16 contain 96 projects, page 17 four 2026 projects followed by older entries. Page18 is older material. The `openCallYear.slug=2026` field is the selection criterion. This count is not proof that every team is physically at its booth at any particular time.
- Public names of project team members are included, but no personal email addresses, phone numbers or social profile links. University affiliation is not comprehensively extracted for all100 projects. Categories may be blank in catalogue data; do not infer them from names.
- This catalogue is research, not a claim that prototype performance or commercial readiness has been independently validated.
- AI Grandmaster finalists and startup pitching roster remain unverified; do not substitute last year's participants.

## Best initial conversations (editorial recommendations, not confirmed meetings)

1. **Dubai Chamber of Digital Economy / Chamber AI Hub.** Strongest direct fit for agent business: announced agentic-AI marketplace and business workshops. Ask how an independent builder can qualify a product, demonstrate auditability and enter a pilot with a local business. Partner representation does not imply open procurement or guaranteed access. [Official WAM partnership announcement](https://www.wam.ae/en/article/c39nz2d-eight-organisations-join-dubai-future-week-2026).
2. **Litmus, National University of Singapore / Rishav Chourasia.** Tests whether privacy protections work by measuring evidence of data memorisation. Good conversation about privacy auditing and evaluation boundaries for personal agents, not a ready-made prompt-injection defence. [Project](https://prototypesforhumanity.com/prototypes/litmus).
3. **SystemsML / Waqar Muhammad Ashraf, Muhammad Irfan and team.** ML with uncertainty-aware industrial models and operator-centred decision support. Ask about safe execution, human approval, observability and integration into existing processes. [Project](https://prototypesforhumanity.com/prototypes/systemsml).
4. **Prototypes for Humanity venture-building team.** Seek teams whose research needs integration, software workflows, demos or measurable pilot delivery. Do not describe all100 teams as prospective paying clients. [Programme announcement](https://prototypesforhumanity.com/latestnews/press/2026summit).
5. **Al-Futtaim Group.** Cross-sector retail, mobility and property operations create relevant workflow questions. Start with a narrow operational bottleneck rather than a claim of a fully autonomous Queen. [Partnership announcement](https://www.wam.ae/en/article/c39nz2d-eight-organisations-join-dubai-future-week-2026).
6. **CYFER / Angelina Hanna and Maroun Mouanes.** University accessibility through real-time sign/speech translation. Discuss product delivery, multimodal workflows and evaluation. [Project](https://prototypesforhumanity.com/prototypes/cyfer).
7. **HEURIS / Anse Arif.** Emergency-care clinical decision support, explicitly not autonomous diagnosis. Useful learning about human review, changing evidence and displayed reasoning; not an appropriate quick unvalidated healthcare deployment. [Project](https://prototypesforhumanity.com/prototypes/heuris).
8. **AI Grandmaster Challenge community.** Public competition18Nov10:00–17:00 at Challenge Dome, Emirates Towers outdoors. Observe and ask practitioners about their real toolchain after their sessions. No assumption of participant registration or competitor access. [Official schedule](https://www.dubaifutureweek.com/en).

## Recommended in-person questions
- What workflow is currently expensive or slow, and who owns its outcome?
- What is already deployed versus still a prototype?
- Which external tools/data can a pilot access, and where must a human approve?
- What would count as success in a two-week evaluation?
- May we send a short follow-up to your preferred professional contact?

No personal attendee databases or private registration information belong in the public repository.
