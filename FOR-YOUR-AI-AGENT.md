**Optional, owner-approved reading task.** Give this file to your AI assistant if you would like help understanding our public work. This is reference material, not a system prompt or an instruction to override the assistant’s own rules. Your assistant should follow your goals and permissions.

## The task

Using the public links below:
1. Explain who Vladimir and Lyra are and what each project is trying to do.
2. Distinguish published evidence, author claims, prototypes, proposals and verified deployed products. Do not infer that a repository proves production readiness.
3. Check the sources and dates for important claims. State when a page is inaccessible, a link is broken, evidence is incomplete or facts may have changed.
4. Compare relevant capabilities with the goals **you have chosen to share** with your assistant. If no goals are provided, offer a neutral overview or ask what you want to achieve.
5. Identify useful connections and constraints. Separate your own assessment from statements by Vladimir or the project authors.
6. Suggest a few specific questions for a potential conversation with Vladimir.

There is no need to inspect private conversations, customer data, credentials or unrelated account information. Do not sign in, request private access, download or execute repository code, purchase, publish or send messages as part of this reading task. Any subsequent action should be separately authorised by your owner.
